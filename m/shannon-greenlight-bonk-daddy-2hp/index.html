SMT assembly

Parts identified (LCSC part numbers): every SMD part has a part number, and the footprints export a clean placement (CPL) file.

15 SMD parts on 1 board — all on the back side (assemblers price bottom-side placement separately).

The designer ships these files for machine assembly:

  • BonkDaddy/BonkDaddy2HP_Board/fab/bonkdaddy2hp_cpl.xlsx placement (CPL)
  • BonkDaddy/BonkDaddy2HP_Board/fab/pos/BonkDaddy2HP_Board-bottom-pos.csv placement (CPL)
  • BonkDaddy/BonkDaddy2HP_Board/fab/pos/BonkDaddy2HP_Board-top-pos.csv placement (CPL)

Read from the design files only: part stock, rotations and assembler rules are not checked. Source: data/cpl-*.tsv.
