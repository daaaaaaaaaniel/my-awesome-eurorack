SMT assembly

Placement-ready: the footprints export a clean placement (CPL) file; not every SMD part has a part number yet.

190 SMD parts on 1 board — all on the back side (assemblers price bottom-side placement separately).

The designer ships these files for machine assembly:

  • main_board/fab/bonkulator_cpl.xlsx placement (CPL)
  • main_board/fab/pos/bonkulator_cpl.xlsx placement (CPL)

The placement file may be older than the board: 2 of its references are not on the current board.

Read from the design files only: part stock, rotations and assembler rules are not checked. Source: data/cpl-*.tsv.
