# 2HPico-Eurorack-Module-Hardware
2HP and 4HP Eurorack modules based on Raspberry Pi RP2350 Pico 2

December 2025 - added Version 1.0 2HPico KiCad design files, BOM, Gerbers for main board and panel board and 3D printable panel.

Jan 28/2025 - added Version 1.0 2HPico DSP PCB design files and FR4 front panel design files and gerbers. 

Feb 16/2026 - JLCPCB assembly files are now verifed. Minor BOM changes to 2HPico DSP as a result of further testing.

April 13/2026 - added 4HPico DSP PCB files. PCB fabbed, tested and working with 1 jumper. 

May 6/2026 - updated 4HPico DSP PCB to V1.1 which corrects missing GND plane connection from middle to bottom of PCB. I have no plans to order these because V 1.0 is fine for me. Changes are minimal so not much risk in ordering V1.1.
Added .stl file for 3D printed panel. No plans to do a PCB panel at this point - I'm happy with the 3D printed panel.

July 2/2026 - added 4HPico PCB files

2HPico, 2HPico DSP, 4HPico and 4HPico DSP are complimentary designs intended for different uses:


The 2HPico has 1 or 2 CV inputs and 1 or 2 CV outputs depending on how its jumpered - see notes below. Its dual DAC CV output(s) can also be used for audio generated by the RP2350 processor. 2HPico can be used to process triggers, gates and CV inputs and can generate CV or audio out - its well suited for sequencers, LFOs,  oscillators etc.

2HPico DSP has an audio ADC and DAC to enable signal processing for audio effects like delays, reverbs, chorus etc. 

2HPico and 2HPico DSP use the same front panel and control panel PCBs.
