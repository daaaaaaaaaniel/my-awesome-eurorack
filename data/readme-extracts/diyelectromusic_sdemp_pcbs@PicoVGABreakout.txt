# Pico VGA Breakout

A VGA signal breakout board designed principly for use with 3V3 microcontrollers, but not exclusively.

- Design and build notes: https://emalliab.wordpress.com/2026/08/01/simplified-pico-vga-part-2/

Bill of Materials:

- Pico VGA Breakout PCB
- VGA socket - PCB Mounted
- Pin headers
- Range of resistors and diodes to taste (see notes above).

Errata:
- The silkscreen details the use of 300R resistors for the RGBY1111 mode, but it requires 330R.

If you like what you see, you can buy me a Ko-Fi - https://ko-fi.com/diyelectromusic

#  A Word of Caution!

Please note - I consider myself a novice at pcb design!

**Don't spend your own time and resources on any of these designs without knowing what you are doing.  They are not "off the shelf products" ready to go!**

Do not use this with expensive computer, audio, music or electronic equipment, it is for hobby use only.  For more details, see https://diyelectromusic.wordpress.com/pcbs/
